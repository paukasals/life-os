Our Story

Nobody was doing this the way it's supposed to be done.

Pau Casals grew up shopping the stalls of La Boqueria in Barcelona — real seafood, no shortcuts, simply normal. A former professional water polo player for the Spanish national team, he came to Miami in 2012. When the pandemic took everything, he looked at what the city was calling a lobster roll and went all-in on building something else entirely.

"There's nobody doing this the way it's supposed to be done."— Pau Casals, Chef-Owner
