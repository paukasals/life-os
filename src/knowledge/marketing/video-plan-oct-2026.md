## Words: approved and banned (everywhere — video, caption, title, comments)

**Approved:** "Wild-caught Maine lobster." "Maine lobster, claw & knuckle
only." "Claw & knuckle only." "Cracked. Picked. Cleaned by hand." "Maine"
(cold, Old Bay mayo) / "Connecticut" (warm, our butter) — both 100% Maine
lobster. "Made to order." "Open until 2am." "Walk up." "4.9 stars. 190+
reviews. All word of mouth." "Our butter" / "house lobster butter" (name
only, never ingredients).

**Never:** fresh, frozen, "caught today", "live lobster", "every night" about
cleaning, "takes 15 minutes", anything about the tail, "most lobster rolls...",
competitor names, "no shortcuts", discounts, "tag a friend", "comment which
one", butter ingredients, staged/scripted reactions, exact review counts
beyond the rounded figure above.

## Posting system (every Reel)
- Cover: a frame with food centered + series name in small cream text.
- Caption, 3 lines: (1) what it is + search words ("Maine lobster rolls in
  Wynwood, Miami"), (2) one detail, (3) location pin + hours.
- 5 hashtags: #lobsterroll #mainelobster #miamifood #wynwood #lobsteria
- Location tag "LOBSTERIA" on every post. Alt text describes the roll in
  plain words.
- Order of posting: Instagram → same-day TikTok (search-style native text)
  → YouTube Shorts → Facebook Reels → best still to Google Business Profile.
- Story same day with a link sticker. Daily 4:30pm Story: "Open tonight
  5pm→2am. Walk up." Reply to every comment/DM in the first 2 hours.
- Trial Reels: post V1/V5 to non-followers only first (24h, max ~5/day or
  the feature locks for 30 days); share to followers if the 3-second hold
  is strong.
