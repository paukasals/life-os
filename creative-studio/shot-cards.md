# Lobsteria Shot Cards — Full Detail

Source: `LOBSTERIA_Camera_Course.pdf` Part B, uploaded 2026-11-25. This is the
authoritative, most detailed version of each video's shot list — richer than
the summaries in `video-plan-oct-2026.md`. A = iPhone on Osmo Mobile 7P,
B = iPhone on Neewer tripod (or overhead clamp where noted).

**Note on "lock WB" (V1, CN):** the native iPhone Camera app — what weeks
1-5 actually use, per camera-course-reference.md's own BASIC/PRO split —
has no manual white-balance lock. "Lock WB" here means the practical BASIC-
level equivalent: one consistent light source for the whole take, so color
doesn't visibly drift, corrected further in editing if needed. A real
numeric WB lock (4000-4500K) needs the Blackmagic Camera app, which that
same file marks as the PRO stage starting week 6 — not needed yet.

## V1 — Cracked. Picked. Cleaned by hand.
LOBSTERIA · The Standard · Mon Oct 12 5:30pm · 12-15s · silent

- **Hook (0-4s):** macro claw crack, shell flying, real crack sound. Clean
  claw meat visible by 3s.
- **Why it works:** satisfaction engine, proves the standard wordlessly,
  loops (next claw lifted → cuts back to the crack).
- **Setup:** B — tripod 45°, 30-40cm from board, 2x lens (or 4x telephoto
  from further back), board lower-middle of frame. A — Osmo on its built-in
  tripod, low/side 15°, 1x, tracking off. Board on counter, tray to the
  right. Back-side light ~135° from the window, black board behind for the
  steel.
- **Settings:** 4K60 both phones, HDR off, lock WB, AE/AF lock on the claw,
  exposure -0.5 to -0.7. Slo-mo 4K120 on B for one crack take. (PRO:
  Blackmagic 1/120, WB 4300K locked.)
- **Sound:** Mic Mini TX 30cm from the board, noise cancelling OFF. Music
  low in the edit.
- **Shots:** 0-2s macro crack (B, slo-mo) · 2-5s pull knuckle meat (B) ·
  5-9s cleaning by hand, gloved (A) · 9-12s meat into the tray (B) · 12-14s
  full tray hold · end frame: the next claw lifted.
- **Text:** 0s "Every roll starts here." / "Cada roll empieza aquí." · 2.5s
  snap: "One claw at a time. By hand." / "Pinza a pinza. A mano." · end:
  "Cracked. Picked. Cleaned by hand." / "La rompemos, la sacamos, la
  limpiamos a mano." ("Wild-caught Maine lobster" goes in the caption.)
- **During:** no gestures. Hold every action 2s before/after. Crack 3-4
  claws for options. Don't move the cameras between takes.
- **Edit:** cut on action; slo-mo crack at 50% (60fps) or the 120fps clip;
  100→112% crop push on the crack; crack sound peaking over the music; loop
  the ending.

## V2 — Claw & knuckle only. Here's why.
LOBSTERIA × @paupau.ownerdiary Collab · Wed Oct 14 · 25-35s · Pau talks

- **Hook:** spoken, staccato, over the claw crack: "Claw and knuckle. That's
  all. It's the more expensive way to make a lobster roll. Here's why we do
  it."
- **Text:** 0s "The more expensive way." / "La forma más cara." · end: "Claw
  & knuckle only." / "Solo pinza y nudillo."
- **Why it works:** curiosity hook plus owner explaining a decision; trust
  from a real person, working hands, one clear reason.
- **Setup:** A — Osmo on the counter, waist-up, eye level, 1x, Airstream
  behind, tracking ON (palm gesture before the first line). B — tripod 45°
  on the board (hands), 2x. Soft light 45° on face, window behind.
- **Settings:** 4K30 both (talking). AE lock on face (A), on board (B).
  (PRO: 1/60, WB matched on both.)
- **Sound:** Mic Mini TX on collar into the Osmo module (up to 2 TX
  directly) or RX in USB-C; noise cancelling ON; windscreen on. Clap once
  at start.
- **Shots:** 0-2s claw crack (B) · 2-10s look up, first line (A) · 10-20s
  claw then knuckle (B) · 20-30s cleaning a piece (B) · 30-35s finished roll
  at 45°, Airstream steel (B or A).
- **Say (EN):** "Claw and knuckle. That's all. It's the more expensive way
  to make a lobster roll. Here's why we do it. It's the sweetest, most
  tender meat on the lobster. Soft. Buttery. It holds together in the roll.
  So we crack every claw by hand, pick every knuckle, clean it piece by
  piece, and check every piece before it goes in. Wild-caught Maine lobster.
  Claw and knuckle. That's the only lobster we use."
- **Say (ES):** "Pinza y nudillo. Nada más. Es la forma más cara de hacer un
  lobster roll así. Es la carne más dulce y tierna de la langosta. Suave.
  Mantecosa. Se mantiene entera en el pan. Por eso rompemos cada pinza a
  mano, sacamos cada nudillo, la limpiamos pieza por pieza, y revisamos cada
  trozo antes de ponerlo. Langosta de Maine salvaje. Pinza y nudillo. Es la
  única langosta que usamos."
- **Delivery:** slow/calm on "Claw and knuckle. That's all"; pause 1s after
  "the more expensive way to make a lobster roll"; lower/closer voice on
  "Here's why we do it"; more energy/pace on the hands-on list; slow down
  and smile on the last line. 5 read-throughs out loud first.
- **During:** say it in your own words, 3 full takes. Pause 1s between
  sentences (cut points). If you reframe: finish the sentence, put tools
  down, gesture, restart.
- **Edit:** A for talking, B on every "show" word (claw, knuckle, clean).
  Captions in the opposite language. Music very low. Collab invite to
  @paupau.ownerdiary when posting.

## CN — Counter Note (standard format for announcements)
LOBSTERIA Story or Reel · 8-12s · silent · every holiday, special opening,
Event Box deadline, closure, "we're open tonight"

- **Why it works:** the handwriting is the hook — people stay to read the
  message as it appears. Repeated with the same spot/marker/tray, it
  becomes a recognizable format.
- **Setup:** B only — phone on the overhead arm, 90° straight down over a
  clean stretch of the Airstream's steel, chive/garnish bins visible along
  the top edge (the "kitchen" signal). 1x, lens 50-60cm from the surface.
  Lay a stainless tray or parchment to write on; light from the side so the
  marker strokes and steel texture show, no glare on the tray.
- **Settings:** 4K30, HDR off, lock WB, AE/AF lock on the writing surface,
  exposure -0.3. (PRO: 1/60 shutter.)
- **Sound:** the real marker squeak + soft kitchen ambience, music low or
  none.
- **Shots:** 0-6s hand writes 2-4 short lines, big clear letters, slightly
  angled like the reference · 6-9s hand leaves, words hold a beat · 9-12s a
  finished roll set on the tray so the key words stay readable, hold 2s.
- **Write (examples):** only what's true that day — "Open tonight / 5pm →
  2am 🦞" · "Holiday Monday? / We're open" · "Event Boxes / order by
  Thursday" · "Last roll 1:45am" · ES: "Hoy abrimos / 5pm → 2am" ·
  "¿Festivo? / Abrimos".
- **During:** write slowly; practice the words twice on scrap paper first
  so the spacing fits. Keep your hand from covering the line just written.
  One take per message; if a letter fails, start again on a fresh sheet.
- **Edit:** speed up the writing slightly (110-130%) if it drags, keeping
  the squeak audible. Caption = the message in the other language + address
  and hours. Post as a Story (daily reminders) or a Reel (holidays, special
  openings).
- **Food safety:** never write on a surface where food goes. Use the tray
  or parchment kept for this, sanitize the counter afterward. The roll sits
  on its own tray, not on the ink.
- **Pass check:** every word readable on a phone at full speed, the roll
  doesn't cover key information, whole clip under 12 seconds.
