## 1. THE DECISION

Launch a **productized dessert-catering line** under The Crepes & Waffles Bar — no live stations at launch (staffing complexity, equipment, setup risk, lines). Instead: centrally prepared, delivered beautiful, served immediately.

**Positioning line (from deck):**
> "Beautiful crepe and waffle desserts, delivered ready for the event — no setup, no waiting, no live station."

**Ownership split:** 50/50 partnership — Pau runs sales/marketing, the pastry chef runs production. Structured as a **profit-share on this division**, not blanket business equity (see `crepes-waffles-catering-partnership-terms.md`).

---

## 2. TWO SELLABLE PILLARS

1. **Dessert Canapés** — Crepe Cups + Mini Belgian Waffle Discs
2. **Celebration Cakes** — Crepe Cakes + Belgian Waffle Cakes

Both pillars share the same creams, toppings, crumbles and sauces → one prep system feeds four formats. Lower operational friction, higher scalability.

## 3. FOUR FORMATS

| Format | Description | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| **Crepe Cakes** | 8–10 thin crepes, paper-thin cream between each, one bold visible filling layer, repeated 3x | 24–30 crepes total, 3 visible sections |
| **Belgian Waffle Cakes** | Golden waffle rounds as "cake layers," cream + fruit between, drips/crumble/sprinkles left visible | Easiest hero product — forgiving, fast to assemble, strong birthday appeal |
| **Crepe Cup Canapés** | Baked crepe cup (crisp shell) → piped mascarpone/ganache/dulce/cream filling → soft crepe rosette or fruit/crumble/drizzle finish | One-hand dessert, ready to platter |
| **Mini Waffle Discs** | 2.25–2.75" mini waffle rounds, cream piped high center, fruit/crumble/cookie bits/sprinkles finish | Discs beat squares: cleaner on trays, full waffle perimeter every bite, no live equipment |

## 4. LAUNCH FLAVOR MATRIX (works across all 4 formats)

**Core (8 flavors, all formats):**
1. Strawberry Pavlova Shortcake
2. Nutella Strawberry Banana *(use banana cream or assemble bananas close to service — oxidation)*
3. Dulce de Leche Churro
4. Cookies & Cream + Chocolate Cookie
5. Birthday / Funfetti
6. Dulcetella — Nutella + Dulce de Leche
7. French Toast — cinnamon + maple
8. Tiramisù

**Premium/seasonal add-ons (+2, all formats):**
- Pistachio Praline Crunch
- Key Lime Pie Pavlova *(Miami-specific add)*

**Rule: core menu + 2 premium adds only. No endless custom flavors at launch.**

## 5. LAUNCH PACKAGE STRUCTURE

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Canape trays | 24 / 36 / 48 pieces. Client picks 2–4 flavors per tray. |
| Cake sizes | One standard size first. Add a larger catering size only after testing transport + holding. |
| Flavor discipline | Core menu + 2 premium seasonal adds. No open-ended customization. |
| Production rule | Build everything around stable creams, thick sauces, crumbles, same-day fruit. |

**Sell trays and cakes — not customization chaos.** This is the operating discipline for pricing, ops, and the landing page alike.

## 6. WHY GOOGLE ADS FIRST, INSTAGRAM SECOND

Google Search captures existing demand ("dessert catering Miami"); Instagram creates demand. Spend the first ad dollar on someone actively searching, not someone scrolling. Full campaign structure in `crepes-waffles-catering-google-ads.md`.

## 7. GO-TO-MARKET SEQUENCE

1. Existing customers (truck/bar regulars) — free, immediate, warmest audience
2. Google Search — highest purchase intent
3. Direct B2B outreach / sample boxes to event planners, hotels, offices — potentially the biggest revenue per hour of sales effort
4. Instagram organic/Reels — brand proof, feeds retargeting pools
5. Meta paid ads — scale once organic proves the creative

Full week-by-week plan: `crepes-waffles-catering-launch-checklist.md`.

## 8. WHAT'S STILL OPEN (needs Pau + chef, not marketing)

- Real pricing per tray size / cake size (food cost + packaging + labor — see the economics workbook)
- Minimum order value and lead time (draft: 24–48h notice)
- Delivery radius and delivery fee vs. pickup-only at launch
- Whether the "larger catering cake size" ships at launch or after transport testing
