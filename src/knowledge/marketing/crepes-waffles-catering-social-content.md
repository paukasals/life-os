# The Crepes & Waffles Bar — Catering Social Content Plan
Last updated: August 30, 2026
*Instagram's job here is different from Google's: it creates demand and builds the retargeting pool Meta Ads will use later. Native vertical video, not menu screenshots.*

## LAUNCH ANNOUNCEMENT SEQUENCE

**Feed post (day of launch):**
Caption: "Now catering. Crêpe cakes, Belgian waffle cakes, and dessert trays — delivered ready to serve. Link in bio."
Visual: hero shot of the full catering spread (deck slide 1 style — cakes + canapé tray together).

**Story sequence (3–5 frames, pin to Highlight "CATERING"):**
1. "We're catering now 🎉" text over b-roll
2. Product close-up (crepe cake cross-section)
3. "How it works" — 3 steps, swipe-up/link sticker to /catering
4. Poll sticker: "Cakes or trays for your next event?"

**Pinned Reel (launch week):** see Script 1 below.

## REEL SCRIPTS

### Script 1 — "Meet the catering menu" (12–15 sec, pin this one)
- 0–2s: finished crêpe cake, full shot
- 2–4s: knife cutting through the layers, cream/fruit exposed
- 4–6s: strawberry filling reveal, close-up
- 6–8s: piping a crêpe cup canapé
- 8–10s: tray of mini waffle discs, overhead pan
- 10–12s: finished catering table, wide shot
- Text overlay: "MIAMI, MEET CRÊPE & WAFFLE CATERING."
- End card: "Cakes · Crêpe Cups · Mini Belgian Waffles — link in bio"
- Audio: trending audio, food-reveal cut style (check Reels trending sounds week-of, don't hardcode)

### Script 2 — "The build" (process, 15–20 sec)
- Waffle rounds stacking → cream piped → fruit placed → drip finish → candles
- Text overlay: "Golden waffle rounds. Cream and fruit between every layer."
- CTA end card: "Birthday cake, but make it Belgian."

### Script 3 — "24-piece tray in 15 seconds" (B2B-leaning, for office/event audiences)
- Empty tray → flavors going in one by one (top-down) → full tray → box closed → handed off
- Text overlay: "24 pieces. 4 flavors. Delivered ready."
- CTA: "Ordering for the office? Link in bio."

### Script 4 — "Which flavor are you?" (engagement/carousel or Reel)
- Quick cuts of all 8 flavors, one per second, with flavor name overlay
- CTA: "Comment your pick — we'll DM you the catering menu."

## CADENCE (first 30 days)

- Feed: 3–4x/week, alternate product / process / occasion / proof
- Stories: daily during launch week, then 3–4x/week — always with the CATERING highlight link
- Reels: 3x/week for launch month, then settle to 2x/week
- Repost every client photo/video (with permission + tag) once real orders start — UGC outperforms produced content for proof

## META ADS (phase 2 — after organic proves the creative, ~week 2–3)

- Start with the best-performing organic Reel as the ad creative (don't produce ads blind)
- Budget: $20–30/day to start
- Objective: Leads (on-platform form) or Traffic to /catering, test both
- Audiences: Miami 15–20mi radius, interests (event planning, catering, birthday party planning) + retargeting website visitors from Google Ads traffic
- Retarget: everyone who visited /catering or /cakes but didn't submit the quote form (7-day and 30-day windows)
