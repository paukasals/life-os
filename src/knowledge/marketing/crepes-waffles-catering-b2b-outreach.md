Last updated: August 30, 2026

*Building the named list itself (names, emails, LinkedIn) is a research task — flag if you want it run as a separate search pass; it needs a live web search, not just this repo.*

## WORKFLOW

1. Build named list (50 accounts) — separate research task
2. Chef preps a batch of tasting boxes (aim for 10–15 for the first wave)
3. Pau delivers/ships with the box card + a handwritten or printed note
4. Follow-up email 3–5 business days later (Template 3)
5. Track every account in one place (CRM or a simple sheet): name, org, sample sent date, follow-up date, status, first order value
6. Repeat wave every 2–3 weeks until the target list is exhausted, prioritizing accounts that reply first

## GOAL

Not 1,000 customers — 10 event planners, 5 wedding planners, 10 buildings, 10 offices, 5 hotels, 10 brokerages that reorder regularly. A handful of accounts each ordering monthly is worth more than one-off retail traffic.
