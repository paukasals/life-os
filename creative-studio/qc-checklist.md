# Creative Studio QC Checklist (condensed)

Distilled from brand-rules.md, camera-course-reference.md and
compliance-notes.md for use in automated per-clip analysis. Full reasoning
and sources live in those files — this is the short list a reviewer (human
or Gemini) checks every clip against.

## Brand-rule risks (reject or flag)
- Says/implies "fresh" or "frozen" about the lobster.
- Calls Pau "chef," or he's wearing a chef jacket.
- Shows or states the lobster butter recipe or shrimp oil recipe.
- Any discount language ("% off," "deal," "special price").
- Shows LOBSTERIA and The Crepes and Waffles Bar together, UNLESS this is
  for @paupau.ownerdiary (that account is the one allowed exception).
- Uses "fresh/frozen," "takes 15 minutes," "every night," competitor names,
  or exact review-count claims beyond "4.9 stars, 190+ reviews."

## Food safety / styling
- Ready-to-eat food (cooked lobster, assembly, caviar) handled without black
  nitrile gloves.
- Visible bare-hand contact with food that's about to be served.
- Hair not tied back/capped near food prep.

## Legal / compliance flags
- A customer or staff member's face is clearly recognizable — is there an
  **on-camera recorded "yes"** to being filmed, in this same clip? That's
  the whole consent standard (Pau's policy, 2026-11-26) — not an
  off-camera verbal okay, not an assumption from context. No recorded yes
  in the clip → flag "do-not-post", regardless of how good the footage is.
- A minor is visible in frame at all — the minor's own on-camera yes is
  NOT enough; flag unless a parent/guardian's on-camera yes is also in the
  clip.
- A Wynwood mural is in frame, in focus, identifiable as a specific artist's
  work — flag for blur/crop unless permission is on file.
- Audible licensed/recognizable music playing in the background of the shot
  (not added in editing) — platforms mute/block this the same as using the
  track directly.

## Technical quality flags
- Visibly blown-out highlights on food (exposure not pulled down enough).
- Mixed color temperatures in one frame (e.g. warm key + cold blue
  background light).
- Shaky/handheld footage where the shot calls for a fixed or gimbal-
  stabilized frame.
- HDR-look footage (washed out, flat contrast) where native/Log + grade was
  expected.
- Food not centered in the safe middle band (top ~15%/bottom ~25% get
  covered by platform UI).

## Virality / hook assessment (top KPI — see camera-course-reference.md)
- Does the first 2-4 seconds have a strong hook (visual ASMR, curiosity
  question, or identity/place statement)? Weak hooks (slow intro, "hi
  guys," static shot with no motion) should be flagged for a re-cut of the
  first few seconds, not a full reshoot.
- Does it fit one of the three content families (The Standard / The Owner /
  The Place) or a known Owner Diary / Story card?
- Is it genuinely good, or just acceptable? Per the V10/OD rule: if it
  isn't genuinely great, flag it as "don't post," not "post anyway."

## Content pillar
Product (food/process) · Story (founder/origin) · Vibe (Airstream/night/
place) · Social Proof (reviews/UGC/milestones). Pick the single best fit.
