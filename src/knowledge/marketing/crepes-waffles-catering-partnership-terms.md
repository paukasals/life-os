# The Crepes & Waffles Bar — Catering Partnership Term Sheet (Draft)
Last updated: August 30, 2026
*Draft discussion points, not a legal document. Have a lawyer formalize whatever you and the chef agree on before real money starts moving through this line.*

---

## STRUCTURE

- **Not** 50/50 equity in The Crepes & Waffles Bar as a whole.
- **Not** 50/50 of gross revenue.
- **Yes:** 50/50 split of **net profit** from the Catering & Cakes division only, tracked as a separate internal P&L: *"The Crepes & Waffles Bar — Catering Division."*

## PROFIT DEFINITION (proposed)

Revenue
minus: ingredients, packaging, delivery, merchant/payment processing fees, refunds, paid advertising (Google/Meta), any outsourced labor for catering production, discounts, direct catering-only expenses
= **Distributable profit** → split 50/50

## PILOT PERIOD (recommended)

- 60–90 day profit-sharing pilot before any permanent structure or equity conversation.
- Track weekly: orders, revenue, division expenses, distributable profit, each partner's share.
- At the end of the pilot: decide together whether to formalize, adjust the split, or scale investment (equipment, dedicated staff).

## OPEN ITEMS TO PUT IN WRITING BEFORE FIRST $50K

- **Brand** — who owns "The Crepes & Waffles Bar" name/trademark if the partnership ends
- **Recipes** — whose IP are the flavor formulations (chef's or the business's)
- **Customer database** — who keeps the catering client list if one partner exits
- **Website / catering page** — stays with the core business regardless of the catering partnership's outcome
- **Photography / content** — usage rights if the chef or Pau ever works with another concept
- **Equipment** — who owns/keeps any equipment bought specifically for catering production
- **Existing business goodwill** — the catering line trades on The Crepes & Waffles Bar's existing brand/customers; that goodwill is not part of the 50/50 split
- **Exit terms** — notice period, wind-down process, and what happens to open orders if either partner leaves
