# Lobsteria Brand Rules (Mandatory)

These rules are non-negotiable and override any conflicting language found in
other knowledge files (brand-strategy.md, lobsteria-master-brief.md, etc.).
Every agent, skill, or pipeline that generates or publishes Lobsteria content
must check against this file first.

Source: direct instruction from Pau, 2026-11-24.

## Product language
- Lobster: **"wild-caught Maine lobster, claw & knuckle only."**
- Never say **"fresh"** or **"frozen"** about the lobster.
- Portion sizes: **4 oz / 6 oz** only.
- Never show or write the lobster butter recipe or the shrimp oil recipe —
  not in captions, not in behind-the-scenes content, not in replies.

## Pau's role
- Pau is the **owner**. Never "chef" — not in copy, not in social handles,
  not in on-camera wardrobe/styling choices.

## Brand name and slogan
- Slogan: **"Home of Miami's Most Famous Lobster Roll."**
- Do not shorten to "Miami's famous ___" alone — that collides with Mason's
  positioning.
- Never mix **LOBSTERIA** branding with **The Crepes and Waffles Bar
  (Tcawbar)** — keep them as fully separate brands in copy, handles, and
  visuals.

## Operational facts (must stay accurate in any copy)
- Hours: **5pm–2am**.
- Address: **144 NE 27th St, Miami, FL 33137**.

## Commercial / platform rules
- No discount-language in any copy (no "% off," "deal," "special price," etc.).
- No paid ads on Instagram/Meta. All social on those platforms is organic only.
  (Google Ads remains paid/search — that's a separate, already-approved channel.)

## Publishing gate
- Nothing publishes without Pau's approval in the dashboard. This applies to
  every pipeline stage, including anything produced by Lobsteria Creative
  Studio.
